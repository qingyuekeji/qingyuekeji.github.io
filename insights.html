Notes on sustainable packaging for buyers.

2026-10-25 · Salad Bowls & Soup Cups with Lids / 圆形沙拉碗 / 汤杯+配盖

Most buyers assume the plastic ban goes after the bowl.

It reaches the lid first. Lids are a different material, often a separate line in single-use rules, and the easiest place for plastic to survive on an otherwise paper pack. So specify the pair: 550–1500 ml round salad bowls, 8–32 oz soup cups, matching lids from the same range, lined for the disposal route your market runs. We supply and export this line from a partner manufacturing base. Send the destination and the fill; we will match lid and base before you approve artwork. #PlasticBan #PaperBowls #FoodservicePackaging #SoupCups #SustainablePackaging
2026-10-22 · ISO 9001

"Scope" is the least-read line on an ISO 9001 certificate, and the one your purchase order depends on. It names the site and the processes audited: converting, printing, packing, storage.

Two suppliers can both show ISO 9001 while only one has the cup line and in-house print inside its scope. Paper converting does not automatically cover a PLA straw line. Match the certificate to the SKU and to the site that ships it. If two quotes both say ISO 9001, we will walk you through the scope line by line. Qingyue exports; the certificate belongs to our partner manufacturing base. #ISO9001 #FoodservicePackaging #SupplierAudit #PaperCups
2026-10-20 · QSR & Fast Food

A 12 oz cup carries about 350 ml. The sleeve that usually travels with it is where the cost hides.

Hot coffee at that volume warms the wall within seconds, so single-wall paper normally ships with a sleeve: another line to stock, another motion at the counter. A hollow cup keeps a thin air layer between two paper walls, so the insulation is part of the product. PLA-lined paper handles hot drinks too, as long as its heat limit is written down. Plastic keeps the incumbent's place until a ban reaches your market. We export the paper options from a partner base. 2026-10-15 · Rolled Rim Paper Sushi Tray / 卷边纸寿司托盘

How long does your sushi sit on display?

One number decides whether paper or plastic is the right tray for you - and it is the number most suppliers never think to ask for. - Under four hours (sushi counter, made to order, event catering): a rolled-rim paper tray holds up, and the rolled edge is what does the work - it keeps the tray rigid when someone lifts it with tongs - Over a full day (chilled deli case, heavy sauce): rigid plastic still beats paper on moisture. Keep it, or move to a coated paper tray and test it before you commit a season to it The part buyers do not expect a supplier to say out loud: if plastic is genuinely the right answer for your format, we will tell you. What paper changes even at four hours is the end of the journey. The tray travels from the cold case into the customer's hands and then into recycling - no liner to separate, no plastic a city may refuse to take. It also displays better: sushi, sides and light meals stay arranged and readable under the case lighting, which is most of what a deli counter is actually selling. Tell us how long your product sits and what goes in it. We will come back with the structure that fits your format, not the one that is easiest for us to sell. #PaperPackaging #SushiPackaging #FoodPackaging #ReadyToEat #SustainablePackaging
2026-10-14 · North America

Catalogue photos make takeout boxes look alike. The difference shows up hours later, once hot, oily food has sat inside.

Before you approve a compostable lunch box line for North America, ask three things: 1. A filled sample, not a flat swatch. Have it packed with hot food, sealed and left to sit; watch the corners and the lock. 2. The board and lining, named in writing. Water-based, PLA and PE linings handle grease differently, and "eco" is not a spec. 3. Proof behind the claim. If "compostable" is printed on the box, ask which standard the material was tested to, and whether the certificate covers that board-lining pair. We supply and export 800–2000 ml lunch boxes from a partner manufacturing base, with your choice of lock and print. Tell us what your kitchen fills them with. #TakeoutPackaging #FoodservicePackaging #Compostable #NorthAmerica
