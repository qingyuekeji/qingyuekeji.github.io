· Middle East

One cup spec does not fit the Gulf.

Coffee, karak and iced drinks share the same menu — the cup has to handle both. • Single wall — light and cost-efficient; best for cold drinks, hot drinks usually need a sleeve. • Hollow / double wall — built-in air insulation, no sleeve needed, the usual pick for hot tea and coffee. • Corrugated / ripple wall — extra grip and texture for events and outdoor catering. • PLA-lined — plant-based and compostable in certified conditions; ask us about heat limits before locking the spec.
