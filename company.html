Company Profile

Qingyue Technology Co., Ltd. is a global manufacturer of eco-friendly food packaging, dedicated to the design and production of biodegradable paper cups, bowls, food boxes, straws, plates and bags. Founded in 2006 and built on a 60,000 m² modern manufacturing park with automated lines, we supply more than 150 countries and regions with stable quality and flexible customisation.

60,000 m² modern park with automated lines, dust-free workshops, and a complete QC laboratory — from raw material inspection to UV-sterilised, oven-dried finished goods.
